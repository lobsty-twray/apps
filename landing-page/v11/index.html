The Hidden Cost of Spreadsheet Workflows

Excel feels free — until it isn't. These are the real challenges businesses face when critical operations live in .xlsx files.

⚠️ Version Control Chaos

+
Multiple people editing Budget_v3_FINAL_really_final.xlsx — sound familiar? Without real version control, you lose data, overwrite changes, and waste hours reconciling. A web app provides a single source of truth with full audit trails.

🔓 Zero Access Control

+
Spreadsheets have no concept of role-based access. Everyone sees everything — financial data, personal info, proprietary formulas. A web app lets you define who sees what, down to individual fields and rows.

🐌 Performance at Scale

+
Hit 50K rows and Excel starts crawling. Complex VLOOKUP chains turn into minutes-long recalculations. PostgreSQL handles millions of rows in milliseconds. Your data shouldn't slow you down.

🔌 No Real Integrations

+
Copy-pasting between systems isn't integration. Real web apps connect via REST APIs — pulling live data from your CRM, pushing updates to Slack, syncing with payment processors. Automatically. Every time.

📱 Not Mobile-Ready

+
Try editing a pivot table on your phone. Spreadsheets weren't designed for mobile. Modern web apps are responsive-first — your team can update data, approve workflows, and review dashboards from anywhere.

🤖 Manual Everything

+
Every formula, every conditional format, every manual email notification — it all breaks eventually. Web apps enable automated workflows: scheduled reports, triggered notifications, calculated fields that never drift.
