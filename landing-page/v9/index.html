FAQ
Questions? I've Got Answers
How long does a typical project take?▾
Starter projects usually take 2–4 weeks. Professional builds run 4–10 weeks depending on complexity. I'll give you a clear timeline during our discovery call — no guessing games.
What if I don't know exactly what I need?▾
That's totally fine — most clients start there! During our free discovery call, I'll ask about your current workflow, pain points, and goals. Together we'll map out exactly what your app should do. No tech knowledge required.
Do I own the code?▾
100% yes. You own everything — the code, the design, the data. It's your app. I can also set up the repo on your GitHub so you always have full access.
Can you work with my existing tools?▾
Absolutely. I regularly integrate with Google Workspace, Slack, Shopify, payment processors, and third-party APIs. If it has an API, I can connect it.
What about hosting and maintenance?▾
I'll help you get set up on reliable hosting (typically $5–20/mo depending on needs). For ongoing maintenance, my retainer plans cover updates, monitoring, and new features as your business grows.
What makes you different from agencies?▾
You work directly with me — the person writing the code. No project managers, no offshore handoffs, no communication gaps. I'm fast, transparent, and genuinely invested in building something great for your business.
