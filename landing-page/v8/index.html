The Problem

Spreadsheets Were Never Meant to Run Your Business

Sound familiar? You're not alone. Most growing businesses hit the same walls.

📊

Version Control Chaos

Multiple copies of "Final_v3_REAL_final.xlsx" floating around. No one knows which version is current.

🔒

No Access Control

Everyone sees everything — or no one can access what they need. There's no middle ground in a shared spreadsheet.

⚠️

One Wrong Formula Breaks Everything

Someone accidentally deletes a row or overwrites a formula. Hours of work gone. No audit trail.

🐌

It's Painfully Slow

10,000+ rows and your spreadsheet takes 30 seconds to load. Filtering is a nightmare. Reporting is manual.

📱

Unusable on Mobile

Try editing a complex spreadsheet on your phone. Pinching, zooming, accidentally overwriting cells. It doesn't work.

🔗

No Integration

Your spreadsheet can't talk to your email, CRM, inventory, or payment systems without brittle workarounds.
