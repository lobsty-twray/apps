Our Story
A Family Tradition of Craftsmanship
Photo: Ray & Sergio

Sergio Villanueva has spent decades mastering the art of painting — not just applying color to walls, but transforming spaces with the precision and care of a true craftsman. As a certified Fine Paints of Europe specialist, he brings old-world technique to every project.

His son Ray grew up watching his father work, learning that great painting isn't about speed — it's about respect for the craft. Together, they built Frida Painting NJ to bring that same standard of excellence to homeowners across New Jersey.

"We don't just paint walls. We take pride in every brushstroke — because your home deserves nothing less."
