The Craftsman

Sergio Villanueva

With over two decades of hands-on experience, Sergio Villanueva has built a reputation as one of New Jersey's finest interior painters. His journey began with a deep respect for the craft — learning traditional techniques, studying color theory, and mastering the art of surface preparation that separates exceptional work from ordinary.


Today, Sergio is a certified Fine Paints of Europe application specialist — one of a select few artisans in the state entrusted with these world-renowned luxury paints. Every project reflects his commitment to perfection: meticulous preparation, expert color consultation, and flawless execution.

Fine Paints of Europe

Fine Paints of Europe represent the pinnacle of paint craftsmanship — hand-made in Holland with the highest pigment concentrations in the world. Sergio Villanueva is one of New Jersey's certified application specialists, trained to deliver the extraordinary depth, durability, and luminous finish these paints are celebrated for.

  • Highest pigment concentration of any paint in the world
  • Hand-crafted in Holland since 1987
  • Exceptional durability — lasts decades
  • Rich, luminous depth of color unmatched by domestic brands
  • Certified application ensures warranty and optimal results
  • Eco-friendly, low-VOC formulations available
